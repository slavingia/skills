● DISCOVER Y - Minimalist entrepreneurs don’t sell - they educate. Selling is a discovery process that gets you to product-market fit, so minimalist entrepreneurs use sales as an opportunity to talk to potential customers one-by-one about their product while simultaneously educating themselves about their customers. It’ s a long game built on relationships, not a one-day , grand opening extravaganza followed by selling to strangers. ● TRANSP ARENCY - Minimalist entrepreneurs tell the truth. The best marketing shows the world who you and your company really are, so share your stories, from struggle to success. If you understand that people care about other people, you can use marketing to educate, inspire, and entertain, which leads to empathy , which leads to community , which leads to sales, which leads to audience, which leads to growth. Plus, this kind of marketing is free. ● GROWTH - Minimalist entrepreneurs own their businesses, they don’t let their businesses own them. To grow , you have to scale your company , but you don’t spend money you don’t have or sacrifice profitability to get there. Try not to make company killing mistakes, but don’t let fear hold you back. You grow by doing things you’re unqualified to do, but you’re also careful not to lose sight of the bottom line (or your own needs) in the process. Most of all, keep the focus on your customers. ● CULTURE - Minimalist entrepreneurs hire other minimalist entrepreneurs. The way you do things may not be for everyone. That’ s okay. Build the kind of company you want to work for , define your values early and often, and tell the world who you are. Like-minded folks will find you. Conventional wisdom about how we work, when we work, and who we work with is changing fast, which means you can make new rules that help you and the people around you be as effective, and as happy , as possible. ● PURPOSE - Minimalist entrepreneurs know that life is about more than just your company . Being a minimalist entrepreneur is about making a dif ference while making a living. Impact isn’t just exchanging code for dollars, though there’ s nothing wrong with making money . The true magic of entrepreneurship is that you and your business can improve the quality of life of many people. And it doesn’t have to be millions; “enough” is what you decide, not a universal benchmark. The eight principles of being a minimalist entrepreneur each map to a function of the minimalist business–and not coincidentally , to the chapters in this book. Each chapter builds on the previous one – just as addition leads to multiplication leads to algebra leads to calculus – until finally , at the end of the book, you’ll be fully equipped to become a minimalist entrepreneur yourself.
