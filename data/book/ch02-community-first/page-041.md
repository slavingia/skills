That blankness is like a blank canvas, or a blank page, or a blank business plan. You want to start a business to solve a problem, but you don’t have any problems to solve. If you’re struggling here (many do) some economics 101 may help. There are only four different types of utility: form utility , place utility , time utility , and possession utility . What can you make easier to understand, faster to get, cheaper to buy , or more accessible to others? [bulleted list] - Place utility: Making the inaccessible accessible - Form utility: Creating something new from existing parts - Time utility: Making the slow fast - Possession utility: removing a middleman [INSER T ART11] 41
