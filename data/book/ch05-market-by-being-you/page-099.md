But never forget: while social media is sexy–and often leads to having millions of followers, it is not the end all and be all of your business. I’ve seen creators with tens of millions of followers fumble, and creators with just a few dozen earn a living multiple times over . That’ s because social media is the top of the funnel. It’s mostly strangers, remember . Most of them are not fans yet, and almost none of them are customers. You still need to convert them, and to do that, you need to get them to commit. [head A] Middle of the funnel: Emails and communities Don’t call it a comeback. Email’ s been here since the very beginning of the internet. And it’ll probably be there until its end. Twitter , YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook can take away your business at any time, by tweaking the algorithms, shutting down your account, or making you pay to show up in people’ s feeds. So even though social media can be incredibly ef fective for gaining distribution, you are building on rented land. That’ s why , as soon as you have social media followers, you should start building an email list. Email is “peer-to-peer .” It gives you a direct line to your customers that isn’t controlled by a private company , an algorithm, or whether you spend money on advertising. And if you have someone’ s email address, it means they consider you a friend, not a stranger . [INSER T ART24] 99
