Remember , since you are running on your own steam, your runway will now last you forever . You will not die, unless you do something stupid. This means you need to hire slowly , not ambitiously . You should avoid irreversible decisions like getting a multi-year office lease. Moving slowly will mean you will have to ship more thoughtfully , and slower too, but over the coming years you will build a phenomenal product and loyal customers. Even though you may see others rocket up far past you on sexy metrics, they will not be able to surpass your product, no matter how much money they raise, and burn. And the odds are you will be around in ten years, and they won’t. Growing sustainably and moving slowly gives you time and space to learn about yourself, your customers, and your market. It will also give you a clear view of the road ahead. You will be able to detect bugs in your product and systems before they af fect your customers. You can test your software in private beta with customers, or behind a waitlist. You can make sure it’ s good enough before you give it a wide release. This way, your customers continue to appreciate every thoughtful addition you make and to love your product without worrying about the mistakes that accompany quick changes and rash decisions.
