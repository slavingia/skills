Startups are encouraged to “fail fast,” and founders often cycle through several teams at the same time they’re cycling through ideas. But there’ s also a lot of truth when people say , “it’s harder to divorce your cofounder than your spouse,” so if you want to give your business the best chance at success, approach the relationship with your co-founder(s) like a marriage.
