In 2019, they raised capital and began a hiring push. “W e made every mistake in the book,” they say . “Once we had the money and were growing quickly , we hired for our wish list, we hired friends and family , and we hired anyone who seemed nice and wanted a job.” The result was what Janessa called a “cultural crisis” during which bullying, gossip, and drama became commonplace. Their first step was to hire a leadership coach and to begin the process of identifying what was happening more clearly . The company went through a 360 review process, and Matt and Janessa started to ask themselves what kind of leaders they were and what kind of leaders they needed to be to manage a growing team. They saw that they had focused too much on making their employees “happy” and not enough time defining what would best serve their customers and provide the best work atmosphere. Secondly , they realized that even though they and many of their customers loved the company , it wasn’t going to be the right fit for everyone. This revelation sent them back to the drawing board to consider their values, which they now define using the acronym CACAO for customer-centric, ambitious, compassionate, adaptable, and ownership. What’ s more, they translated those values into a list of attributes that describe the type of person who will thrive at Simply Eloped, and they now use that list for their job postings. With respect to hiring itself, Simply Eloped instituted a more clearly defined process with multiple interviews and standardized questions. As a result, the company now attracts leaders that are better able to exemplify and model the company’ s values day-to-day . Creating and living the company values has been so transformative that Janessa, Matt, and their team use them frequently to highlight success and to give feedback; during the weekly announcements, Janessa uses company values to tell stories about employees’ s small wins; on the flip side, if someone isn’t performing well, she can use the values as context for how and why to improve. Values have ended up being so important at Simply Eloped that Matt and Janessa even wrote a song about them that every employee learns. While you may not want or need to write a song with your company values (we definitely are never going to write a song at Gumroad), it’ s worth emulating how clearly Janessa and Matt articulate and define the company’ s values, for themselves and for everyone who works there.
