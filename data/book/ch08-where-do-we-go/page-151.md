I believe strongly that the arc of humanity is in its infancy , and the way we make progress is via mindful business creation. One key reason I’ve used the word “minimalist” to describe entrepreneurship in this book is because I believe your business does not need to be the answer to every question. Becoming a minimalist entrepreneur may result in a great outcome for you while also creating opportunities for your team and your community . But it is unlikely to solve every problem you encounter , either for yourself and for the world. And I think that’ s okay .
