# Gumroad's Values — Reference

Gumroad operates with 4 values. Each is non-obvious, backed by stories, and used in daily decisions.

## 1. Judged by the Work

What matters is the experience creators and customers have.

- "Everything we send to creators is of the highest quality, meaning everything is reviewed by multiple people"
- "We are okay with employee churn if it helps us ship a superior product"
- "It should be considered a failure to receive feedback on something that could have made a creator's life better AFTER you shipped"

**The test**: quality of output over hours worked, titles held, or meetings attended.

## 2. Seek Superlinearities

A function that eventually grows faster than any linear one. Applied to time and impact.

- "We have a fixed number of hours, and an unlimited amount of creator income to actualize"
- "Every day you are producing superlinear returns on your time investment"
- People may outgrow their role and leave to start their own company — that is celebrated, not mourned

**The test**: is this work compounding, or is it linear effort for linear output?

## 3. Everyone is a CEO

Ownership and autonomy at every level.

- "You are the CEO of your function, and it is your responsibility to make sure it is executing at a high level"
- "Think like a CEO asking for approval from their board, not like an employee asking their manager for direction"
- "If someone needs to ask you how things are going, they are not going well"

**The test**: does this person need to be told what to do, or do they tell you what they are doing?

## 4. Dare to Be Open

Information symmetry as a competitive advantage.

- "If there's a Gumroad secret, it's this one: we aim for complete information symmetry"
- Make onboarding documents public, share financials on Twitter
- Disclose everyone's salary to the whole company
- No meetings, no secrets, no FOMO

**The test**: if this information leaked, would it matter? If not, share it proactively.

## Simply Eloped — CACAO Values

Another example from the book: Simply Eloped uses the acronym CACAO to make values memorable:

- **C**ustomer-centric
- **A**mbitious
- **C**ompassionate
- **A**daptable
- **O**wnership

The acronym format helps with recall. Each value has stories and hiring interview questions attached.

## Key Takeaway

Both examples share traits: values are specific enough to guide decisions, backed by stories (not slogans), and used in hiring and feedback daily.
