## Core: The Profitability Equation

**Profit = Revenue - Costs.** Profitability gives you infinite runway, clarity, and control. When you're profitable, you move at your own pace — not someone else's.

- **Variable costs** (COGS): scale per unit — payment processing, hosting, fraud
- **Fixed costs**: don't scale — domain, hosting, people. People are always #1

## Cost-Cutting Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Pay yourself as little as possible, at first | Sahil started at $36K/year in SF. Went to $0 when things got rough. |
| Hire software, not humans | Pilot, Bench, Gusto, Zapier. Software is cheap; people are expensive. |
| No office until remote proves insufficient | Offices create massive associated costs. Get one as a reward, not a starting point. |
| Stay where you are | Don't move to SV. Lower costs = faster profitability. |
| Outsource before hiring | You + robots → freelancers → employees. In that order. |

## Burnout Prevention

Two fatal mistakes: running out of money, and running out of energy.

**Co-founder relationships** — treat like a marriage. Discuss early: what does success look like? An exit? How fast? Why together? Use vesting. Have hard conversations before they get harder.

**Personal sustainability** — don't treat it as all-or-nothing. Lots of real estate between beach lifestyle and 24/7 grind. Hire when it hurts — a mature business absorbs new people better.

## Growth Mindset

- You don't need to dominate or disrupt. The longest-lived businesses are often the smallest.
- Gumroad grew 87% in 2020 without working more hours. Growth follows customers, not effort.
- Others may rocket past on vanity metrics. They won't be around in 10 years. You will.

## Output

For any business decision, evaluate:

| Lens | Question |
|------|----------|
| Profitability impact | Does this improve or hurt the equation? |
| Reversibility | Can you undo this if it fails? (Avoid leases, big hires, VC terms) |
| Customer-driven vs ego | Are customers asking for this, or is it vanity? |
| Simpler alternative | Is there a cheaper/simpler way? |
| Default alive or dead | After this decision, which are you? |
