# Gotchas — grow-sustainably

Common failure modes when advising on sustainable growth.

| Gotcha | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| VC reflex before profitability | Bootstrap first, fundraise from strength. Raising money before profitability means losing leverage and control. |
| Office before remote proves insufficient | Offices create massive associated costs (furniture, utilities, commute culture, lease lock-in). Remote is the default — office is a reward. |
| Hiring before it hurts | Premature hiring = premature complexity. Wait until the pain of NOT hiring is obvious. A mature business absorbs new people better. |
| Ignoring co-founder relationship conversations | Treat it like a marriage. Undiscussed expectations about growth pace, exits, and roles poison everything later. |
| Working more hours = faster growth | Gumroad grew 87% in 2020 without working more hours. Growth follows customer demand, not founder effort. |
| All-or-nothing thinking | There is a lot of real estate between "beach lifestyle business" and "working 24/7 grind." Most sustainable businesses live in the middle. |
| Copying funded competitors' spend patterns | They are burning investor money. You are spending your own. Different games with different rules. |
