# Fundraising Alternatives

When traditional VC is not the right fit — or you want to raise from a position of strength.

## Bootstrap-First Principle

Profitability gives you leverage in any fundraising conversation. When you don't need the money, you negotiate better terms, retain more equity, and keep control. Shopify and 1Password raised VC only after being profitable for years.

## Alternative Funding Sources

### Earnest Capital
- Invests in bootstrapped, profitable businesses via SEAL (Shared Earnings Agreement with a Lid)
- No board seats, no forced exits. Returns capped at a multiple of investment.
- Best for: founders who want growth capital without giving up control
- https://earnestcapital.com

### Indie.vc
- Revenue-based financing model (returns tied to revenue, not equity events)
- Designed for companies generating revenue but not yet venture-scale
- Focuses on sustainable, founder-friendly terms
- Note: program has evolved — check current terms

### TinySeed
- Accelerator for bootstrapped SaaS companies
- $120K for 10-12% equity (terms vary by batch)
- No relocation required, remote-first
- Includes mentorship from experienced SaaS founders
- https://tinyseed.com

### Regulation Crowdfunding
- Turn customers into investors, aligning stakeholders
- Gumroad raised via crowdfunding on March 15, 2021 — their community invested because they believed in the product
- Platforms: Republic, Wefunder, StartEngine
- Cap: $5M/year under Reg CF (US)
- Pros: marketing event + fundraise, aligned investor base, no single investor has control
- Cons: admin overhead, ongoing reporting requirements, many small shareholders

## When to Bootstrap vs Raise

| Signal | Bootstrap | Raise |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Revenue covers costs | Yes — keep going | Not yet needed |
| Growth rate is customer-limited | Stay bootstrapped | Maybe — if capital unlocks distribution |
| Competitors are funded and outspending you | Often fine — big fish eat big fish, not small fish | Consider if market is winner-take-all |
| You need to hire ahead of revenue | Dangerous either way — be sure demand is real | Raise only if unit economics are proven |
| You want to retain control | Bootstrap | Raise only with founder-friendly terms |

## Key Principle

Raise money because you can, not because you must. Profitability means you choose when, how much, and from whom.
