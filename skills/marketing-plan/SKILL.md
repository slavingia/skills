## Prerequisites Check

Before building a plan, verify the user has:

- A community they belong to (if not → redirect to `/find-community`)
- A product people are paying for (if not → redirect to `/mvp`)
- ~100 customers with repeat purchases (if not → redirect to `/first-customers`)
- Experience selling one-on-one (sales informs marketing messages)

If any are missing, name the gap and suggest the right skill. Do not build a marketing plan on a shaky foundation.

## Core Principle

**Marketing is sales at scale.** Start by spending time, not money. Blog posts, social media, email — all free. Only spend money after organic traction proves your message works.

## The Marketing Funnel

Every customer passes through all five stages. You cannot skip steps:

1. **Engage** — They encounter your content (social, blog, word of mouth)
2. **Follow** — They find you interesting enough to subscribe/follow
3. **Research** — They check your product/website
4. **Consider** — They evaluate pricing, features, alternatives
5. **Buy** — They become a customer

## Community vs Audience

| | Community | Audience |
|---|-----------|----------|
| Direction | Multi-directional (members talk to each other) | One-to-many (you broadcast) |
| Your role | Fellow member | Creator/leader |
| Size | Smaller, deeper | Larger, wider reach |
| Example | Slack group, subreddit, local meetup | Email list, Twitter followers |

Your community is a subset of your audience. Build audience to attract strangers → fans → customers.

## Content Levels

Progress through three levels, each reaching a wider audience. See `references/content-levels.md` for detailed framework with examples.

1. **Educate** — Share what you learned building the business (easiest, start here)
2. **Inspire** — Share your journey, struggles, vulnerability (builds connection)
3. **Entertain** — Humor, storytelling, pattern-breaking (hardest, widest reach)

## Tool Integration

| Step | Tool | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Content research | `web_search_exa` / `web_search_advanced_exa` | Find what content works in the user's niche — search for indie creators AND big brands |
| Platform analysis | `perplexity_search` / `perplexity_ask` | Research which platforms their audience actually uses, posting frequency norms |
| Competitor content | `firecrawl search` | Analyze competitor blogs, newsletters, social presence |

If tools are unavailable, continue in advisory-only mode.

## Email: Own Your Audience

Social media is rented land. Email is owned land. Always build an email list alongside any social strategy. See `references/content-levels.md` for email-specific guidance.

## Output

Help the user create:

1. Their primary content platform and posting schedule
2. 5 content ideas for each level (educate, inspire, entertain)
3. An email list strategy (what to offer, how to collect)
4. A "build in public" plan — what to share from their journey
5. When (if ever) to consider paid advertising

End with concrete next actions for this week.
