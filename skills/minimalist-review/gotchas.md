# Gotchas — minimalist-review

Common failure modes when reviewing decisions through the minimalist lens.

| Gotcha | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Applying all 8 principles with equal weight | Prioritize based on the user's current stage. A pre-revenue founder needs principle 4 (Sell Before Scale) more than principle 7 (Grow at Customer Speed). |
| Dogmatic minimalism | Sometimes spending money IS the right move — after organic validation proves the channel works. Minimalism is a starting point, not a religion. |
| Reviewing in isolation without asking stage | A post-revenue SaaS and a pre-launch side project need completely different advice. Always ask first. |
| Giving generic advice without business context | "Build less" is useless without connecting to the user's specific product, market, and constraints. Ground advice in their situation. |
| Assuming every decision needs radical simplification | Some complexity is earned. A business with 1,000 paying customers has different needs than one with zero. |
| Missing the meta-question | Before evaluating HOW to do something, ask: is this the right decision to be making right now? Sometimes the answer is "stop deliberating and ship." |
